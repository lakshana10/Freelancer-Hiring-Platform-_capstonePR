Freelancer Hiring Platform Problem statement
Freelancers and clients today rely on scattered, informal channels (social media, personal contacts) to find work or hire talent, leading to unverified profiles, no structured way to compare bids, unsecured payments, and no centralized tracking of contracts, communication, or reviews. There is a need for a unified online platform where clients can post jobs, freelancers can bid with proposals, both parties can securely manage contracts and payments, and trust is maintained through ratings and reviews — streamlining the entire hiring process from job posting to project completion.
